# Hero images

Drop the rotating hero photos here, named in order:

    hero-1.jpg
    hero-2.jpg
    hero-3.jpg
    ...up to hero-8.jpg

- Numbering must be continuous (the page stops at the first missing number).
- Lowercase `.jpg` only.
- Portrait, roughly 4:5 (e.g. 1200 × 1500 px), under ~400 KB each.
- Keep the person's face in the upper-middle of the frame; the bottom-left
  corner is covered by the floating message card.
- With no images, a branded green panel is shown instead.

Images crossfade every 6 seconds.
